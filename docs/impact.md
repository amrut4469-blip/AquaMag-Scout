# 🌍 Impact & Benefits

| Area | Benefit |
|---|---|
| 💰 Economic | Lower survey cost; more areas can be screened with the same budget |
| 🔬 Scientific | Better seabed mapping and targeted data collection |
| ⚙️ Operational | Plug-and-play on ROV / AUV, no vehicle redesign |
| 🛡️ Strategic | Supports the Deep Ocean Mission |
| 🌱 Environmental | Targeted sampling means less seabed disturbance |
| 🇮🇳 Technology | Indigenous sensor (Atmanirbhar Bharat) |

## Beneficiaries
- Research institutes and ocean technology agencies
- Survey operators with ROV / AUV fleets
- Students and labs that need a low-cost seabed sensor

## How Success Will Be Measured
- Cost of the pod compared with conventional sensors
- Area surveyed per dive
- Time taken to screen a site
- Detection and false-alarm results from trials

## Long-Term Potential
- Sensor array for wider coverage
- Mounting on lander and autonomous platforms
- Dataset that improves classification over time
